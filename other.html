Screenplays, Music And Poetry

Aside from drawing, writing and music composition is another passion of mine. My experience is very amateuristic and I'm not very knowledgeable in regards to theory, but my love of being busy is my main driving force and I'm eager to learn. I won the poetry competition at my highschool threefold, hosted school events and designed the school's annual Christmas cards. I was pretty reliable even during exam period and frequently got commissioned by the secretary to do these sorts of things. I practised piano during breaks with a friend, and have only used free music programs with limited features up until now. I was an active member of my schools art club and would write or draw there almost all week.
